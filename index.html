The challenge

One theme. Four practical missions.

Learners work with Word, Excel, PowerPoint and basic HTML while creating their own fictional Stickables collection. They can invent characters, names, special powers and artwork.

For teachers: Download the resources below and share them with your class. The instructions are written in simple, direct steps to help learners work independently.

Your mission briefing

Choose a mission and get started

Each mission has a clear goal, a short list of tasks and a starter file. Work through the steps in order and save your work with your name in the filename.

Mission 01

📄 Word: Create a Catalogue

Turn a basic document into a neat, colourful character catalogue.

Modules 3.1–3.7
  1. Save a copy of the starter file using your name.
  2. Edit the text and format the headings and paragraphs.
  3. Create a table with at least four characters.
  4. Add at least three pictures, icons or shapes.
  5. Check the layout and save your finished file.

Mission 02

📊 Excel: Analyse Sales

Use formulas and a chart to find out which characters are popular.

Modules 4.1–4.4
  1. Save a copy of the workbook using your name.
  2. Calculate total sales, target difference and bonus points.
  3. Complete the summary using spreadsheet functions.
  4. Create and title a chart comparing units sold.
  5. Answer the questions using your results.
Download Excel starterXLSX ↓ Teacher formula guideDOCX ↓
Mission 03

🎤 PowerPoint: Pitch It

Present your characters and convince people why they are fun to collect.

Module 5.1
  1. Open the four-slide starter presentation and save a copy.
  2. Introduce three or four characters.
  3. Add a chart and two facts from your Excel work.
  4. Give two reasons to collect the characters.
  5. Check the slideshow and save it.

Mission 04

💻 HTML: Build a Website

Create a simple web page to introduce your fictional collection.

Module 5.2
  1. Open the starter file in a text editor, such as Notepad.
  2. Change the page title and main heading.
  3. Add two paragraphs and a list of three characters.
  4. Add an image and keep it in the same folder as the HTML file.
  5. Save the file and open it in a browser to test it.

Quick start

How to use this resource

Keep it simple. You can run the challenge over several lessons or select the missions that fit your teaching plan.

1
DownloadSave the mission posters and the starter files you want to use.
2
SharePost the links or files on Teams, Moodle or your school's learning platform.
3
CreateAsk learners to follow the numbered steps and save their own copies.
4
CheckUse the checklist and teacher guide to review the finished work.
Suggested pacing: Lesson 1 — Word; Lesson 2 — Excel; Lesson 3 — PowerPoint or HTML. If you need to assess every module, allow additional time for the final mission.
Assessment idea

A flexible way to assess the project

The included teacher guide suggests a 100-mark framework. Adapt it to your school's assessment requirements and the time available.

30 marksWord catalogue

Editing, layout, graphics, table and formatting.

35 marksExcel tracker

Formulas, summary functions, chart and analysis.

25 marksPowerPoint or HTML

Clear communication, correct features and design.

10 marksCreativity and care

Originality, readability and file management.
